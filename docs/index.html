ICARUS-LEO

A simulator of in-network caching over LEO satellite constellations

This site accompanies the paper Understanding In-Network Caching for LEO Satellite Networks: A Physical-Layer Perspective by G. Campobello and M. Amadeo. ICARUS-LEO extends the Icarus in-network-caching framework to content delivery over LEO satellite constellations.

The pages below are the interactive part of the material. The full repository, with the documentation and the licence, is on GitHub.

Prepare a simulation

  • Config builder — describe your scenario and download the configuration file
  • User guide — how to fill it in, send it, and read what you receive back

Worked examples

Each example reproduces a result reported in the paper. The session viewer shows the delivery of individual chunks, one at a time, with the events along the path: cache or origin hits, losses, retransmissions and handover interruptions.

  • Case study 1 — four caching strategies on a dynamic constellation with a lossy channel
  • Case study 2 — a real burst-loss trace against a memoryless model, with and without retransmissions

The configurations that produced them are in the repository, under docs/examples.
